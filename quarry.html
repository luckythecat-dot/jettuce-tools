Content Strategy

Jettuce Game Quarry

The Sweet Spot: Games with a strong premise, recognizable aesthetic/personality, or weird cultural footprint—that don't require 50 hours of mastery to understand.

Leans heavily into 2000s/early-2010s PC + childhood internet culture, with a specific focus on Philippine internet café nostalgia (O2Jam, Ragnarok Online, Audition) as a unique secret spice.

🟡 1. Mini Retrospective / Analysis

Requires a focused, specific thesis question rather than a broad history lesson.

PopCap Games (Goldmine)
Simple mechanics make it easy to talk about why they work without needing 50 hours of footage.
  • Peggle: "Why Was Peggle So Addictive?" / "Why Does Peggle Feel Like a Perfect Video Game?"
  • Zuma Deluxe: "Why Were We All Addicted to Zuma?" / "The Game That Turned Matching Colors Into Gambling"
  • Insaniquarium: "Why Was Insaniquarium So F***ing Weird?" / "The Strangest Game PopCap Ever Made"
  • Bookworm Adventures: "Why Did This Word Game Have So Much Personality?"
  • Plants vs. Zombies: "Why Does Plants vs. Zombies Still Feel So Good?"

🟡 2. Retro / Niche Game Investigation

High potential lane connecting game history with specific Philippine internet-café culture.

O2Jam
Combines personal context + PH computer shop nostalgia + rhythmic gameplay.
  • "Why Did Everyone in the Philippines Play O2Jam?"
  • "I Went Back to the Internet Cafe Game That Raised Me"
Audition Online
Rhythm game meets early internet social network/dating culture.
  • "The Internet Cafe Game That Was Basically a Dating App"
  • "Why Was Audition Online So Popular in the Philippines?"
Ragnarok Online & Gunbound
Massive titles that need a single focused angle rather than a full history lesson.
  • "I Finally Played the Game My Internet Cafe Friends Were Obsessed With"
  • "Why Did Everyone at the Computer Shop Play Gunbound?"

🟢 3. "I Played the Weirdest Games"

Core premise: "I cannot believe someone made this." The game does half the comedic heavy lifting.

  • Hatoful Boyfriend ➔ "I Played the Pigeon Dating Simulator"
  • Jazzpunk ➔ "What the F*** Is Jazzpunk?" (Tailor-made for observational humor)
  • I Am Bread ➔ "I Played a Game Where You Are Literally Bread"
  • Octodad ➔ "I Played a Game Where Nobody Notices I'm an Octopus"
  • Untitled Goose Game ➔ "I Played a Game About Being an Absolute Menace"
  • Goat Simulator ➔ "I Played Goat Simulator in 2026 and I Understand Nothing"

🟢 4. The 2000s Casual-Game Rabbit Hole

Exploring the ecosystem around Diner Dash rather than single-game challenge videos.

Ecosystem Games
Cake Mania, Wedding Dash, Sally's Salon, Cooking Dash, Farm Frenzy, Virtual Villagers, Plant Tycoon, Fish Tycoon.
  • "Why Were 2000s PC Games So Addictive?" (Diner Dash ➔ Cake Mania ➔ Zuma ➔ Peggle)
  • "Why Did Every 2000s Game Have a Tycoon Version?" (Zoo Tycoon, Fish Tycoon, Hospital Tycoon)
  • "Why Did Every Game Need a Sequel?"

🟡 5. Franchise / Ranking & 🟢 6. Flash Era

Franchise & Ranking Concepts
  • "I Played 5 PopCap Games From My Childhood and Ranked Them"
  • "I Went Down the Big Fish Games Rabbit Hole"
  • "I Played Every 'Dash' Game From the 2000s"

Emerging Channel Identity

"I find games with a story attached to them." (Not necessarily an in-game story, but the cultural/meta story around the game):

  • "Why was this so popular?"
  • "What happened to this?"
  • "Why is this game insane / How did this get made?"
  • "I'm going to play this ridiculous thing and see what happens."
