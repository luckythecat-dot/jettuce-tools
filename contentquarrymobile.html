Editorial Framework

Jettuce Content Philosophy

Our videos bridge nostalgic casual gaming, internet pop culture, and analytical depth. Here are our core editorial tenets.

1. The High-Culture Lens

Treat casual games (PopCap, Flash games) with the same narrative seriousness as AAA masterpieces.

2. Immediate Hook Discipline

Hook the viewer in the first 8 seconds with a specific premise or core memory instead of vague intros.

3. Emotional Nostalgia

Connect gameplay mechanics directly to childhood environments (after school, internet cafés, family PCs).

4. Fast-Paced Visual Editing

Keep visual rhythm active with UI zooms, retro SFX, archival soundbites, and snappy motion graphics.
